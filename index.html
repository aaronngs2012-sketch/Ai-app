Questions?

Frequently asked

What materials do you print with?

Mostly PLA (great all-rounder), PETG (stronger and heat resistant), TPU (flexible and rubbery) and resin (very fine detail for miniatures and jewellery). Not sure which you need? Pick “Not sure” on the custom form and we'll advise.

How long does shipping take?

Shop items ship within 1–2 business days. Custom orders typically ship 3–5 business days after you approve the quote. Rush options are available.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Yes! Send photos, a sketch with measurements, or the broken part's dimensions and we can model it for you. Design time is included in your quote.

Can I change the colour of a shop item?

Most shop items let you choose a colour before adding to cart. For colours not listed, use the custom order form and choose “Personalise a shop item”.

What is your returns policy?

Shop items can be returned within 30 days in original condition. Custom items are made to order and can't be returned, but if something arrives damaged or doesn't match the approved quote we'll reprint it free.
