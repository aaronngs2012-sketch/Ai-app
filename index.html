Questions?

Frequently asked

What materials do you print with?

Mostly PLA (great all-rounder), PETG (stronger and heat resistant), TPU (flexible and rubbery) and resin (very fine detail for miniatures and jewellery). Not sure which you need? Pick “Not sure” on the custom form and we'll advise.

How long does shipping take?

Shop items ship within 1–2 business days. Custom items typically ship 3–5 business days after we confirm your order.

How is a custom item priced?

The custom form shows an estimated price based on size, material, finish and quantity. We check every custom order and email you if the final price is different before you pay.

Where can I see what I ordered?

Open the Orders tab. It lists every order and quote request you've placed on this device, with the items, prices and date.

What is your returns policy?

Shop items can be returned within 30 days in original condition. Custom items are made to order and can't be returned, but if something arrives damaged we'll reprint it free.

    Set up publishing (one time)
    1. On GitHub, open Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token.
    2. Name it “Shop publishing”, set an expiry, and under Repository access choose Only select repositories → Ai-app.
    3. Under Permissions → Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
    4. Tap Generate token, copy it, and paste it below.

    The token is saved only on this device. Treat it like a password.
